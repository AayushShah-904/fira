How to cite this pack

The same three tiers, read as actions.

Cite Current & dated — safe to quote today

  • FCNR is the strongest variant. Axis (38, 6 ccy, with RBI lock-in flags), RBL (30), IDFC (30), Bank of Maharashtra (21), AU (25), ICICI (13, thin), Saraswat & Cosmos (USD-led) — all carry a June 2026 effective date.
  • Indian Overseas & IDFC NRE/NRO — freshly scraped and bank-dated (May/June 2026).
  • GIFT-City: ICICI (24, 8 ccy) and Canara (32, 3 ccy) — dated and multi-currency.

Caveat Usable, but flag the caveat when quoting

  • Undated ladders — Axis, AU, ICICI, RBL, BoM NRE/NRO carry no bank-published effective date. Scraped fresh, but the bank didn't stamp a date, so treat the "as-of" as the scrape date, not an official one.
  • RBL GIFT (84 slabs) — the largest GIFT ladder, but undated. Rich, cite the count with the "as-of scrape" caveat.
  • IOB FCNR (45, 9 currencies) — broadest FCNR currency set, but undated.
  • BoM GIFT (7, USD only) & Yes NRO (84 days old) — thin / aging; fine as directional.

Doubt Verify before citing — stale dates or under-extraction

  • Yes Bank NRE & FCNR — the source PDF stamps an effective date of 2019. Either a misprint on a current card or a stale archive; do not quote until the PDF is confirmed current.
  • Canara Bank NRE / NRO / FCNR — all three carry an effective date of 1 July 2025 (a year old). The site is JS/WAF-protected, so the pipeline may be reading an old cached card.
  • Cosmos NRE (eff 2020) & Saraswat NRE (eff Mar-2025) — clearly stale rupee-deposit rates for these co-ops.
  • IDFC GIFT (9, was 22) & Axis GIFT (12, was 24) — both are under-extracted from dated GIFT-City PDFs that have since rolled; the fuller ladders are known and being recovered. This is the one documented quality-gate exception in the pack.

What is genuinely missing

  • Yes Bank GIFT — Yes operates a GIFT-City IBU, but its page carries no static rate table (FX-market-linked), so no slabs.
  • NRO for BoM, Saraswat, Cosmos — not published as a separate schedule; for these banks NRO is typically priced at the domestic term-deposit rate.
  • GIFT for AU, IOB, Saraswat, Cosmos — these institutions do not operate a GIFT-City IBU, so GIFT is not applicable (not a gap).

Method & provenance. Rates are extracted from each bank's own website / rate-card PDF (never aggregators), collected via an automated cloud run on 8–9 July 2026 and assembled into this pack. NRE and NRO are rupee-denominated (no currency column); FCNR and GIFT are foreign-currency. "Effective date" is the date the bank printed on the card; "fresh" means scraped within the last day. A quality gate blocks any bank that loses >50% of its rates versus a fixed baseline — one exception (IDFC GIFT) was accepted and is documented above. Counts are slab-level (one bank · currency · tenure = one slab).
