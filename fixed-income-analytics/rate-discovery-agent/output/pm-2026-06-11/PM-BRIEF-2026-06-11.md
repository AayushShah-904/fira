## Headline

The pack reflects the best snapshot per (bank, variant) chosen across:

- the current scrape on disk
- git HEAD (last verified committed state)
- the 2026-06-09 cohort raw output
- prior published packs (2026-05-14, 2026-04-16)

**Decision rules** (correctness-first, not slab-count):
- **Fill empty slots** only from `extraction_confidence='high'` snapshots scraped within 90 days.
- **Replace existing data** only when the alternative is **same-or-higher confidence**, has **≥ as many slabs**, and is **strictly fresher**.

We never trade slab count for freshness, and we never replace verified disk data with older or lower-confidence alternatives.

## Caveats for the PM

1. **134 banks still carry no scraped_at timestamp** — these are HEAD-restored co-op / DCCB / RRB banks where the most recent live scrape attempt regressed and we held prior committed state. Real rate data, but freshness is "as of last commit," typically weeks old.
2. **5 FCNR banks are 31–90 days old** (DBS, South Indian, Cosmos Co-op, CSB, HDFC). The HDFC FCNR is the weakest at 73 days / unknown confidence — worth a targeted re-scrape before citing.
3. **41 destructive overwrites were blocked** during the 2026-06-09 cohort by the safe-write hardening shipped in Sprint 010 Phase J+K. Without those blocks the pack would have regressed further.
