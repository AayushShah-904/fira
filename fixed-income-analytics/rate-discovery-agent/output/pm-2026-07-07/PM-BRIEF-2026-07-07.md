## Why an interim delivery

The RBI FCNR concession window (deposits opened/renewed **8 Jun – 30 Sep 2026**) makes NRI
rates time-sensitive. This pass shipped the sprint-011 NRI fast path: per-currency
extraction fixes (JPY near-zero rates no longer dropped), automatic fallback when banks
roll their dated PDF URLs, effective-date capture, and four data-protection rules that
route suspect overwrites to operator review instead of clobbering good data.

### RBI lock-in flag (new this delivery)

Axis Bank's FCNR slabs now carry `regulatory_flag_keys` on the 3–5 year tenors plus a
verbatim `regulatory_constraints` note: deposits opened/renewed 8 Jun–30 Sep 2026 with
3–5y contractual tenor carry a **mandatory 1-year lock-in**. Other banks publish the same
RBI condition — capture currently lands only on image/PDF-extracted banks; HTML-path
parity is queued. Treat 3–5y FCNR rates in the window as lock-in-bearing when citing.
