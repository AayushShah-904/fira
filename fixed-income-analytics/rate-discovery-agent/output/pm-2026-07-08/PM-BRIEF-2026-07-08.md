### RBI lock-in flag

Axis Bank's FCNR 3–5y slabs carry `regulatory_flag_keys` + a verbatim
`regulatory_constraints` note: deposits opened/renewed **8 Jun–30 Sep 2026** with 3–5y
contractual tenor carry a **mandatory 1-year lock-in**. Other banks publish the same RBI
condition; capture currently lands on image/PDF-extracted banks (HTML-path parity is
queued). Treat 3–5y FCNR rates in the window as lock-in-bearing when citing.

## Caveats & known headroom

- **GIFT/IBU is on older data.** The 18 GIFT banks were **not** in today's cohort, so
  their scrapes are 31–90 days old (none ≤30d). The rates are real and complete, just not
  freshly refreshed this cycle — cite with that caveat. A GIFT-focused cohort is the fix.
- **~13 banks have a richer version pending review.** A colleague is reviewing 20
  local-vs-cloud discrepancies; ~13 are NRI-relevant (a few NRO/NRE/GIFT banks where a
  fuller ladder exists locally). None blocks this pack; promoting them enriches a future
  one. (7 of the original set were already auto-restored into this pack.)
- **2 banks on fallback data** (`slice-small-finance-bank`, `indian-bank-ibu`) ride
  last-good local data because their cloud extractor currently fails — data is correct,
  just not cloud-refreshed. Extractor fixes are queued.
